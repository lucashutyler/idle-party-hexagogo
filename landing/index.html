What makes it different

An idle game that is actually multiplayer

Most idle games are solitaire with a leaderboard bolted on. This one is built so that every class is worse alone.

Always running

Combat resolves on the server, one tick per second, forever. Closing the tab does not pause your party — it just stops you watching.

Weak solo, strong together

A Bard alone is nearly harmless. In a party, Rally lifts everyone's damage by 20% per member. Every class is designed to make the others better.

Always in a party

You are never partyless. Play solo and you get a solo party; join friends and you fight the same monsters together, positioned on a shared 3×3 grid.

Nobody online? Hire help

Henchmen are recruitable NPCs that fill empty party slots, so a thin friends list never means playing at a disadvantage.

A world to uncover

A hex map under fog of war, with zones to unlock, dungeons to delve, shops, quests and item-gated rooms that stay shut until you have the key.

One character, one story

One character per player, by design. No alt armies, no reroll treadmill — every hour you spend goes into the same hero.

Getting started

Playing takes about a minute

No client to download, no launcher, no payment details.

  1. Sign in with email

    Enter an address and follow the link we send. No password to invent or forget.

  2. Choose a class

    Knight, Archer, Priest, Mage or Bard. One character, so pick the role you want to play.

  3. Find a party

    Invite friends, join a guild, or hire henchmen. Position everyone on the combat grid.

  4. Close the tab

    Your party fights on without you. Come back for loot, levels and a combat log of what you missed.

Questions

Before you start

Is Idle Party RPG really free?

Yes. The game is free to play in any modern browser, and there is nothing to buy to reach any part of the content.

Do I have to leave the game open?

No, and that is the point. Combat is resolved on the server, so your party fights, travels and earns whether or not you are connected. You can close the tab for a day and come back to everything that happened.

Do I need friends to play?

No. You are always in a party, even solo, and you can recruit henchmen — NPC party members — to fill the empty seats. Friends make you stronger, but the game does not stall without them.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. It is built mobile-first and works in a phone browser. You can also install it to your home screen and run it like a native app, notifications included.

How many characters can I have?

One. Every system is designed around a single character, so there is no advantage to running alts and no pressure to.

Is there a download or an installer?

No. It runs entirely in the browser. Installing it to your home screen is optional and uses the browser's own install prompt — there is no file to download.
